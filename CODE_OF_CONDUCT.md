# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FloriaApps is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in our projects and community.

## Expected behavior

We expect contributors and community members to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively.
- Accept different perspectives.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, and technical problems.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Respect the decisions of project maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or deliberately hostile behavior.
- Any other conduct that would make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this code of conduct is violated.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

- Removing inappropriate content.
- Asking someone to stop inappropriate behavior.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers of the relevant project.

Please provide enough information to help us understand what happened.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to FloriaApps repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other official project spaces.
